# CAC hymn text sources and rights notes

## Included collections

- CAC Gospel Hymn Book (CAC GHB): 152 English records in the current photographed index, drawn from [HymnFlow’s CAC GHB data](https://github.com/ebena107/HymnFlow/blob/master/hymn-bundle/cac_ghb.json).
- CAC Yoruba Hymn Book (CAC YHB / Iwe Orin CAC): 997 Yorùbá records from [HymnFlow’s CAC YHB data](https://github.com/ebena107/HymnFlow/blob/master/hymn-bundle/cac_yhb.json).

The English and Yorùbá collections are kept separate. Their numbering does not provide a reliable translation mapping, so the app does not pair records by number. The member app and admin use the current photographed index of 152 English hymns; the former 1,000-record English import is no longer part of the active catalog. Meter-only English headings use the first lyric line as the displayed title when available. Records flagged as part of a Various section use a V suffix to distinguish intentional repeated printed numbers. App-internal IDs are unique and are not printed hymn numbers.
