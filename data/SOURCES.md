# CAC hymn text sources and rights notes

## Included collections

- CAC Gospel Hymn Book (CAC GHB): 1,001 English records from [HymnFlow’s CAC GHB data](https://github.com/ebena107/HymnFlow/blob/master/hymn-bundle/cac_ghb.json).
- CAC Yoruba Hymn Book (CAC YHB / Iwe Orin CAC): 997 Yorùbá records from [HymnFlow’s CAC YHB data](https://github.com/ebena107/HymnFlow/blob/master/hymn-bundle/cac_yhb.json).

The source files are kept as separate collections. Their numbering does not provide a reliable English–Yorùbá translation mapping, so the app does not pair records by number. The app displays 1,000 distinct English hymns from the 1,001 GHB source records: source hymn 110 repeats hymn 86 and is merged into that entry, with 110 retained as a search alias. The 602 English records whose source heading is only a hymn meter use the first lyric line as the displayed title. Records flagged as part of a Various section use a V suffix in the app to distinguish intentional repeated printed numbers. App-internal IDs are unique and are not printed hymn numbers.
