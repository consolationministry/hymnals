Simple to get started

Install the Android hymnal.

Download the signed app, then follow Android’s on-screen installation steps.

1

Download the app

Tap Get our hymnal app above and wait for the APK download to finish.

2

Choose “Open with”

In your browser’s download notification or Downloads list, tap the APK and choose Open with.

3

Confirm installation

If Android asks, allow this browser to install apps. Review the installer prompt and tap Install.

Android controls the final prompt. If “Open with” is not shown yet, wait for the download to finish and open the APK from Downloads. Android will ask you to confirm before installing.
